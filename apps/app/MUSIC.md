# Connect — Music Video Brief

A pop track telling the story of a generation born at the singularity. **The lyrics already exist** — we use them as the audio bed for the animated JS music video on the landing page and in the `/hero-video` tab.

> Track length: ~3:30 — ~4:00
> Narrative: birth 2026 → childhood → 10 → 20 → 50 → 2026 again
> Voice: Female, warm, narrative with occasional choral hooks. Reference: Hozier meets Lorde meets Radiohead-Kid A.

---

## The central image metaphor: Connect is the singularity

**The Connect logo is an event horizon** — two arcs falling inward, black center, everything pulled in. But the singularity in this track is not just the brand — it is the *whole* story:

- **Black center** → the point where time, work, and knowledge collapse
- **Orange arcs falling inward** → the curve that keeps getting steeper
- **Asymmetric** → because the future is never symmetric, every generation inherits a different one

### Where the metaphor appears in the video

| Song phase | Visual |
|---|---|
| Birth / Autumn 2026 | Single spark, paper clearing, cold |
| "the year I learned to walk" | First spiral, very slow |
| "they finished jobs" | Spirals accelerate, orange light |
| "machine found a pattern in proteins" | Particles form a DNA helix, collapse into the center |
| "we learned to read them" | Center briefly turns transparent — the inside of an AI |
| "no human did anything better" | Full collapse, black hole fills the frame |
| "we decided who we'd be" | White ray from the center, new beginning |
| "ships halfway to other stars" | Particles explode outward, reversed |
| "you're standing at the steep part" | Camera zooms into the logo, looping back to the start |

## Mood-board for the visual language

**DO NOT** — avoid:
- Kitsch-generic sci-fi (white rooms, blue lights)
- Pixar-becoming-human-and-machine symbols
- Generic "AI eye" tropes

**YES** — the direction:
- P5-brush paper-grain aesthetic (from the original repo)
- Papercut shapes that fold and unfold
- Warm orange gradients with ink/paper black
- Hand-drawn charts and curves
- Sparse, then dense — the faster the narrative, the fuller the frame
- Type as architectural element, not subtitle

**K-pop inspired** in camera movement: hard cuts, clean compositions, but warm instead of cold.

## Hook strategy for Twitter

The video must hold attention in the first 3 seconds:
1. **0:00** Black screen, a single orange spark → curiosity
2. **0:03** "I was born in the autumn of 2026" in large, hand-drawn type
3. **0:08** "Nobody told my parents I was being born at the steep part of the curve" → plot promise
4. **0:30** Hook 1 with the logo → brand moment
5. **3:30+** Final build → emotional payoff

The type *must* be huge, legible, and visually interesting in the first 5 seconds. That is the Twitter retention formula.
