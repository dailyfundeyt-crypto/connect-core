# ElevenLabs — Voiceover Prompt for
# "What is the Purpose of Life?" (50-second hand-drawn collage)

This file gives you three things:

1. **Voiceover script** — drop straight into ElevenLabs Speech Synthesis.
2. **Time markers** — what the viewer sees while each line is spoken.
3. **Voice & delivery settings** — recommended ElevenLabs voice + sliders.

The animation is **50 seconds**. The script is **~138 words**, paced for a calm,
thoughtful delivery that lands at roughly 50 seconds with the sliders below.
If yours runs longer or shorter, adjust `stability` and `similarity_boost` first;
tweak `style` last. ElevenLabs gives you the timing per word — read it against
the markers and re-render the matching section if a line drifts.

## 1. The Script (paste into ElevenLabs)

> People have been asking this for a very long time.
>
> Money, maybe. Fame. A family. Power. Success.
> …and none of them feel quite right.
>
> The clock keeps ticking. The hourglass runs out.
> You look around and wonder — was any of it enough?
>
> Maybe it isn't a destination.
> Maybe it's something quieter than that.
>
> Maybe it's a quiet cup of coffee.
> Someone's hand in yours.
> A sky that someone painted just for tonight.
> The laugh of a child who doesn't know they matter — yet does.
>
> You don't find the answer.
> You make it.
>
> …and then you live it.

## 3. Voice & Delivery Settings

**Recommended voice (ElevenLabs library):**
- `Daniel` (calm, slightly British, male) — best for philosophical narration
- `Adam` (deep, warm, American) — alternative if you want a podcast feel
- `Matilda` (warm, soft, female) — alternative if you want a gentler tone
- `Charlie` (conversational male) — works if the channel leans informal

**Model:** Eleven Multilingual v2 (or the latest available).

**Sliders (start here, adjust to taste):**
| Setting | Value | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Stability | **0.45** | Enough variation to feel human, not so much that it breaks the contemplative mood |
| Similarity Boost | **0.70** | Holds the voice together; not stiff, not noisy |
| Style Exaggeration | **0.20** | A touch of personality — pause before "you make it" |
| Speaker Boost | **on** | Slight clarity lift |

**Pacing notes:**
- Add a comma after "very long time" (already in the script) — ElevenLabs reads it as a breath.
- The ellipsis at "…and then you live it" should land with about a one-second pause before it. If your version rushes, insert a comma: "…and then, you live it."
- The 40:00–46:00 silence is intentional — let the music carry the landscape. If you'd rather fill it, the line "So breathe." appears on screen; you can record it once and drop it in at low volume.

**Output format for re-mux:**
- Export the voice as a single **WAV, 48 kHz, 16-bit, mono** (or stereo if you EQ the music underneath). That gives ffmpeg the cleanest input for final assembly.
