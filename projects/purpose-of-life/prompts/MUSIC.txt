# Music Prompt for
# "What is the Purpose of Life?" (50-second hand-drawn collage)

Two prompts below — pick one, or generate both and duck between them.

---

## Prompt A — INSTRUMENTAL UNDERLAY (recommended)

**Use:** Drop under the ElevenLabs voiceover at -18 to -22 dB. Music swells
slightly during the silent landscape section (40–46s) so the video doesn't
feel empty there.

**Style tags (Suno v4 / Udio):**
```
cinematic indie folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed drums,
warm upright bass, gentle piano, celesta, lo-fi vinyl crackle,
inspirational, hopeful, contemplative, slow build, cinematic,
no vocals, no lyrics
```

**Negative prompt (if your tool supports it):**
```
no vocals, no heavy drums, no distortion, no EDM, no rap, no autotune
```

**Length:** Generate a 60-second piece — Suno's free tier is 60s and you'll
trim the tail. Use **extend** if you need exactly 50s and your first
generation lands short.

**Tempo / key guidance (paste into the "lyrics" or "style" field if
your tool accepts it):**
- Tempo: **78 BPM** (slow but not funereal)
- Key: **D major** (warm, hopeful — natural guitar key)
- Structure: 4 bars intro → 8 bars verse mood → drop to piano + bass
  for the contemplation → gentle build with brushed snare →
  resolve on a single piano note + soft cello underneath

**Reference tracks (mood board, not for Suno itself):**
- Bon Iver — "Skinny Love" (the gentleness)
- Novo Amor — "Anchor" (the build)
- Ludovico Einaudi — "Nuvole Bianche" (the piano resolves)
- Iron & Wine — "Flightless Bird" (the folk warmth)

---

## Prompt B — FULL VOCAL TRACK (if you don't want to do voice yourself)

Only use this if you skip ElevenLabs entirely. Quality of voiceover usually
sounds better with ElevenLabs, but Suno v4 vocals are now genuinely usable
for short-form.

**Style tags:**
```
indie folk, soft male vocal, acoustic guitar, brushed drums, contemplative,
hopeful, lo-fi warmth, gentle build, cinematic short film
```

**Lyrics (paste into Suno's "lyrics" box — note the [Intro]/[Verse] tags):**

```
[Intro - fingerpicked guitar, soft]
[Verse - gentle, half-whispered]
People have been asking this for a very long time
Money, maybe — fame — a family — success
None of them feel quite right
[Verse - lower, slower]
The clock keeps ticking, the hourglass runs out
You look around and wonder — was any of it enough
[Chorus - lift slightly, hopeful]
Maybe it isn't a destination
Maybe it's something quieter than that
[Verse - intimate]
A quiet cup of coffee, someone's hand in yours
A sky that someone painted just for tonight
The laugh of a child who doesn't know they matter
Yet does
[Outro - resolve softly]
You don't find the answer, you make it
Then you live it
[End - single piano note]
```

**Length:** Generate 60s, trim the head/tail in your DAW or in ffmpeg
(`-ss 3 -to 53` to chop 3s off the front and 4s off the back).

---

## FFMPEG one-liner: combine video + voiceover + music

After ElevenLabs generates `voice.wav` and your music tool generates
`music.mp3` (or `music.wav`):

```bash
# 1. mix voice + music with sidechain-style ducking (music quieter when voice plays)
ffmpeg -y \
  -i out/purpose-of-life.mp4 \
  -i voice.wav \
  -i music.mp3 \
  -filter_complex "
    [1:a]volume=1.0[voice];
    [2:a]volume=0.35,afade=t=in:st=0:d=2,afade=t=out:st=46:d=4[music];
    [voice][music]amix=inputs=2:duration=shortest:dropout_transition=0[mix]
  " \
  -map 0:v -map "[mix]" \
  -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k \
  -shortest \
  out/purpose-of-life-FINAL.mp4
```

If you'd rather not script ffmpeg, just drag both files into DaVinci Resolve,
CapCut, or Premiere, lay them on the timeline, and export. The voice markers
in `prompts/ELEVENLABS.txt` line up exactly with the video.
