# Short-Form Animation Channel — Monetization Plan

Short-form hand-drawn animation is one of the **few saturated-but-still-winning
formats** on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels right now. The reason
is supply/demand: anyone can do talking-head video, almost nobody can do
high-quality hand-drawn collage at 30+ second length on a steady cadence.
That scarcity = watch time = money.

Below: how the money works, which categories earn the most, and a 30-video
calendar for the first three months so you can ship one a day without burning out.

---

## How money actually comes in

You don't need a million subscribers. You need **watch time** and **niche
authority** so the algorithm shows your video to the right people.

| Revenue stream | What it pays for | Typical CPM (US/EU) |
|----------------|------------------|---------------------|
| YouTube AdSense (long-form) | 8+ minute videos, mid-roll ads | **$4–$14** for "self-improvement / philosophy" niches |
| YouTube Shorts Fund / Shorts ad-share | Vertical shorts, any length | **$0.01–$0.07 per 1,000 views** — small but stacks fast |
| TikTok Creator Fund + bonuses | Vertical shorts, ≥1 min | **$0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 views** (varies wildly by region) |
| Instagram Reels bonuses | Vertical shorts | **$0.10–$1.00 per 1,000 views** during promo windows |
| Brand deals (mid-tier) | 50k+ followers in a clear niche | **$200–$2,000 per post** in self-improvement, mindfulness, "soft life" |
| Direct sponsorship / licensing | Companies pay for "explainer" style videos | **$500–$5,000 per sponsored short** once you have a portfolio |
| Etsy / Gumroad template packs | You sell the animation "look" as a Notion or video template | **$5–$30 per sale, recurring** |

**Realistic 90-day math** (assuming 1 video/day, ~50% hit, niche audience):
- 30 videos × 100k average views = 3M total views
- Shorts-only revenue: **$60–$210** from AdSense + $1,200–$3,000 from TikTok fund
- If 1 in 10 videos breaks 1M views and goes viral: **+$1,500–$8,000** on that single video
- If you build a recognizable "look" → 2–4 brand deals in 90 days = **$1,000–$4,000**

So a realistic floor for the first quarter is **$2,000–$6,000**, with a real
shot at $10k+ if one piece hits.

The hard part isn't the algorithm — it's **consistent output at this quality**.

## The categories that earn (ranked)

I ranked these by **RPM (revenue per thousand minutes watched)** in the
self-improvement / animation niche on YouTube long-form, and by **organic
reach** on TikTok / Reels. Philosophy alone is fine but slow — these adjacent
niches hit harder.

### Tier 1 — earn the most, organic reach is real

**1. "How AI Works" — explained in 60 seconds**
- *Why it wins:* Every tech creator, every business account, every curious
  adult is searching "how does AI work" right now. CPM is **2-3x philosophy**
  because advertisers are tech/SaaS companies paying top dollar.
- *Examples:*
  - "How a neural network learns — in 50 seconds"
  - "What an AI actually does when you prompt it"
  - "Why AI forgets — and why it doesn't really"
  - "What 'training' an AI actually means (no math)"
  - "How an AI sees the difference between a cat and a dog"
  - "Why AI hallucinates — drawn in 60 seconds"
  - "What is a token? — the unit AI thinks in"
  - "How does ChatGPT pick the next word?"
  - "What an AI is bad at — and why"
  - "Self-driving cars, drawn in 60 seconds"
- *Style:* Same hand-drawn collage. Brain icon, neuron doodles, paper notes
  with terms like "weights", "loss", "gradient", crossed out red herrings,
  a small character trying to teach a robot to recognize a cat.
- *Hook line on-screen (first 1.5s):* "How does AI actually think?"

**2. Short philosophy that ends with a punchline**
- *Why it wins:* Same vibe as your "purpose of life" video but **specific
  questions** that people actually search for. CPM still strong. Watch time
  is huge because people share these to friends.
- *Examples:*
  - "Why do we procrastinate?"
  - "Why are we afraid of silence?"
  - "What is loneliness, really?"
  - "Why do we chase things we don't even want?"
  - "Why is small talk so hard?"
  - "What does it mean to be alive?"
  - "Why do we dream?"
  - "Why do we cry at music?"
  - "What is courage?"
  - "What does it mean to be brave?"
- *Hook line:* "Why do we [search term]?"

**3. Human stories — micro biopics, 50–60s each**
- *Why it wins:* "Story" content has the highest organic reach on TikTok and
  Reels in 2026. People will watch a 50-second story about a stranger more
  than a 50-second essay. And "real human" content is what brands want to
  sponsor.
- *Examples:*
  - "The loneliest man in the world — a true story"
  - "The woman who talked to strangers for 30 days"
  - "He mailed a letter every day for a year. Here's what happened."
  - "She lived alone on a mountain for 7 years"
  - "The last letter his father wrote"
  - "She photographed every sunrise for 1 year"
  - "The night shift worker nobody noticed"
  - "He wrote one sentence a day for 30 years"
  - "She walked the same street for 1,000 mornings"
  - "The man who lived in a library"
- *Style:* Same hand-drawn collage, but biased toward "scrapbook / journal"
  aesthetics — postcards, stamps, journal entries, polaroids, a figure
  drawn as a simple silhouette.
- *Hook line:* "[Pronoun] did [unusual thing] for [length of time]. Here's what happened."

**4. "Soft life" / slow living / solitude**
- *Why it wins:* Aesthetic + emotional. Strong on Instagram Reels. Brands in
  wellness, candle, tea, journaling, books, stationery **pay premium** for
  this aesthetic.
- *Examples:*
  - "A morning alone, drawn in 60 seconds"
  - "What silence sounds like"
  - "The art of doing nothing — for 60 seconds"
  - "Why you should keep a notebook"
  - "The slow joy of waiting"
  - "A room of your own"
  - "Why walking alone clears your head"
  - "The lost art of writing a letter"
  - "How to sit with a feeling"
  - "The pleasure of finishing a book"
- *Hook line:* "What [gentle noun] actually does to you."

### Tier 2 — strong, smaller but loyal audience

**5. Strange-but-true facts, hand-drawn**
- *Why it wins:* Fact content is a TikTok evergreen. The hand-drawn twist
  separates you from every other faceless account.
- *Examples:* "The loneliest whale in the ocean", "Why the sea is salty",
  "What happens to your body in 30 days of silence", etc.

**6. Book / film summaries, 50–60s**
- *Why it wins:* Evergreen search traffic. Affiliate revenue from book
  links compounds over years.
- *Examples:* "Man's Search for Meaning in 60 seconds", "The Alchemist
  in 60 seconds", "Atomic Habits in 60 seconds", etc.

**7. "What if…" thought experiments**
- *Why it wins:* Pure curiosity bait. Watch time goes through the roof.
- *Examples:* "What if you stopped lying for a week?", "What if everyone
  told the truth for 24 hours?", "What if money didn't exist?"

### Tier 3 — niche but compounding

**8. Sleep stories / calming animation**
- *Why it wins:* People loop these to fall asleep. View counts inflate.
  Brand deals with mattress / meditation apps.
- *Examples:* "A walk through a quiet forest", "An old bookstore at midnight".

**9. Micro-history, 50–60s**
- *Why it wins:* "Did you know" content is a TikTok evergreen. Hand-drawn
  collages work beautifully for old paper / archive feel.

## Posting cadence that actually compounds

- **YouTube Shorts + TikTok + Instagram Reels:** same video, all three
  platforms, same day. Use the platform-native uploader (don't link
  cross-platform — algorithms punish cross-posted watermarks).
- **Long-form YouTube (optional):** once a month, take the 5 best shorts
  of the month, stitch them into a 5-minute "episode", post on a Sunday.
  The shorts act as trailers for the long-form; the long-form earns
  10× the ad revenue.
- **Best posting times (US/EU mixed audience):**
  - Tue / Wed / Thu — best engagement
  - 11 AM ET and 7 PM ET — algorithm peaks
  - Saturday morning — surprisingly strong for soft-life content

## Production workflow (so you don't burn out)

You now have a **rendering pipeline that takes 3 minutes per video** and a
prompt system. The hard work is the script and the storyboard. Once a
script is written:

1. **Open** `src/index.html` in your editor.
2. **Add a new beat function** in the same style as `drawBeat1`–`drawBeat6`.
3. **Add it to the `renderFrame` call list** in the right z-order.
4. **Run** `bun run all` (renders + encodes).
5. **Drop the MP4 into your editor**, add voice + music.
6. **Upload to all three platforms** with the hook line as the caption.

That's a 1-video-per-day cadence at professional quality if you batch
the scripts on Sunday for the week.
